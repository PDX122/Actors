# Flashback Actors

Record actors inside a Flashback replay, then direct their performance on the timeline. Use camera clips, morphs, skins and looks without recording the take again.

These guides cover **0.11.2**, currently a release candidate. The [changelog](changelog.md) records what changed; the [test log](test-log/) includes successful runs, earlier failures and screenshots.

## Start here

1. [Download your Minecraft build](downloads.md), then [install it](install.md) with Flashback and Fabric API.
2. [Record your first take](takes.md). Set up the actor, perform, then stop recording.
3. [Add a camera mode](camera-modes.md) and [export the scene](export.md).

![Camera modes library with the Shoulder preview](assets/screenshots/camera-8.png)

## Direct the scene

[Camera modes](camera-modes.md) offers 24 shot presets, including ice boating, horse riding and cinematic reveals. Drag a preset onto an actor, then move or trim the camera clip.

[Timeline editing](timeline.md) covers arranging takes, working with clips and protecting tracks with locks. [Actors Studio](studio.md) keeps scene, editing and looks controls in a dockable panel.

## Change the cast

Use the [Morph library](morphs.md) to choose a body and pose, [Skin studio](skins.md) to apply a skin, and [gear or emotes](gear-emotes.md) to dress and animate an actor.

[Custom models](custom-mobs.md) can use supported Blockbench/GeckoLib geometry and animations. Supported living entities from installed mods can also appear in the Morph library.

## Finish the shot

Add [Looks and effects](looks.md), write [captions](captions.md), or enable [voice recording](voice.md). [Settings and UI themes](settings.md) control recording, playback and the editor's appearance.

If something fails, start with [troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md). Saved test results and their limits are available in the [test log](test-log/).
