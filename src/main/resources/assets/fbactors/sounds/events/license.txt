Explosion source: "Explosive.flac" by qubodup / Iwan Gabovitch
https://freesound.org/people/qubodup/sounds/189778/
License: CC0 / public domain. The source page identifies the recording as
extracted from a United States Government video of explosive disposal.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Adaptations: separate impact recordings were trimmed and normalized; a
low-frequency rumble was derived by slowing, filtering and reverberating the
recording. Ogg Vorbis encodings are bundled for the in-game sound mixer.

fall.ogg: original synthesized falling whistle / rushing air effect, created
for this mod and licensed under the same MIT license as the source code.

nuclear.ogg: "S28-35 Atomic bomb explosion. then rumble.wav" by craigsmith
https://freesound.org/people/craigsmith/sounds/674884/
CC0 https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Vintage Hollywood effects from USC Cinema archival tape; filtered and encoded.
Not an authenticated nuclear test recording.
Runtime procedural pressure audio is original MIT-licensed mod code.
